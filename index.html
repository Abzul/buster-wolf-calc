About Terry's Moves

Buster Wolf is Terry Bogard's Super Special Move in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, originating from Garou: Mark of the Wolves. It's a "hitgrab" — Terry dashes forward and, if he connects, detonates a massive explosion.

Move data:

Input: ↓ ↘ → ↓ ↘ → + A/B (simplified: ↓ → ↓ → + A/B)

Availability: Only when Terry has 100%+ damage (GO! meter active)

Damage: 5% (initial hit) + 20% (explosion) + 15% (collateral) = 40% total

BKB: 73 | KBG: 63 | Angle: 0° (horizontal)

Armor: 8% damage-based armor on frames 1-15

KO potential: As early as ~35% at the ledge, before ~120% from center stage

Buster Wolf only works on the ground. If the opponent shields, the hitgrab doesn't grab and Terry is left at -45 frames disadvantage.

This calculator uses the exact SSBU knockback formula:

kb = ((p/10 + p × d / 20) × 1.4 × (200 / (weight + 100)) + 18) × (KBG / 100) + BKB

Where:

p = opponent's pre-hit % + 20 (Buster Wolf damage)
d = 20 (base damage)
weight = character weight (135 Bowser → 62 Pichu)
BKB = 73, KBG = 63

After raw knockback, we apply:

1. Rage multiplier (1.0× at 100% → 1.1× at 150%)
2. DI factor (1.0 no DI / 0.87 optimal DI)

The calculator uses binary search between 0-200% to find the exact percent where knockback exceeds the stage's blast zone threshold.

Rage is the knockback bonus Terry gets from his own damage. Since Buster Wolf is only available at 100%+, rage is always a factor for this move.

Kill confirms (true combos):

F-tilt → Buster Wolf: The main confirm. Works pre-tumble at mid percents. DI-dependent — opponent can DI down and tech to escape.

D-tilt → Buster Wolf: True from ~60-90% depending on weight. A single D-tilt hit confirms directly.

D-tilt → Jab 1+2 → Buster Wolf: Works at lower percents. The easiest confirm to execute.

Key tips:

Buster Wolf is unsafe on shield (-45 frames). Don't throw it out raw.
Buster Wolf beats Wonderwing (Banjo), Revenge (Incineroar), and Rebel's Guard (Joker) because it's a hitgrab.
If the opponent is prone (on the ground), the initial hit won't trigger the grab — you'll get minimal damage and be heavily punished.

You can cancel the initial hit of Buster Wolf into Triple Wolf (Final Smash).

The famous "Are you OK?" line Terry says when starting Buster Wolf has a surprising origin. A child at a theme park in Los Angeles saw Yasuyuki Oda (SNK designer) exit a water ride soaking wet and asked if he was okay. The moment became an inside joke at SNK and was eventually recorded as the voice clip for Terry's most iconic move.

During development of Garou: Mark of the Wolves, Buster Wolf was originally going to be called "God Twin Geyser".

Power Geyser is Terry Bogard's second Super Special Move in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. A massive vertical explosion that launches opponents upward — useful for ceiling KOs.

Move data:

Input: → ↘ ↓ ↙ ← + A/B (simplified: → ← ↓ → + A/B)

Availability: Only when Terry has 100%+ damage (GO! meter active)

Damage: 26% (clean), 23% (mid), 20% (far)

BKB: 100 | KBG: 41 | Angle: 70° (vertical)

KO potential: Ceiling kill — depends on blast zone height. Generally kills ~15-30% later than Buster Wolf at the ledge.

Power Geyser's vertical launch means stage position matters less — the ceiling height is the same from anywhere on stage. The calculator uses the clean hit (26%) for all values.

Kill confirms:

F-tilt → Power Geyser: Works at higher percents than Buster Wolf confirms. DI-dependent.

D-tilt → Power Geyser: True at certain percent ranges. A reliable confirm.

Jab 1+2 → Power Geyser: Works at low-mid percents. Easy to execute.

Key tips:

Power Geyser is a strong tech chase tool — use it when you read a getup option.
Unlike Buster Wolf, Power Geyser is not a hitgrab, so it can be shielded. Use confirms to make it safe.
The clean hit (26%) is the only hitbox that matters for kill power — make sure you're close enough.

Power Geyser first appeared in Real Bout Fatal Fury 2 (1995) as Terry Bogard's Super Special Move. Unlike Buster Wolf (a physical rush punch), Power Geyser is a ground-based energy attack that creates a massive vertical pillar of fire.

In the Fatal Fury series, Power Geyser was originally a command move (not a Super), performed with ↓ ↙ ← + C. It gained its Super status in later installments.

Together, Power Geyser and Buster Wolf represent Terry's two signature techniques — fire and fist — making him one of the most iconic fighting game characters of all time.
